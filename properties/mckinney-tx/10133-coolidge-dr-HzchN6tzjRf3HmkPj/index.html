About this McKinney rental

Located in an established McKinney neighborhood and served by highly sought-after Frisco ISD, this home offers convenient access to nearby shopping, dining, schools, parks, and major roadways. This spacious 4-bedroom, 3-bath home offers approximately 2,689 square feet of comfortable living space in the desirable Heights at Westridge community. The well-designed two-story floor plan provides plenty of room for everyday living, entertaining, working from home, or simply relaxing. Generously sized living areas offer flexibility for today’s lifestyle, while the multiple bedrooms and three full bathrooms provide added convenience for family and guests. The second floor offers a nice size media room or it can be used as a game room. Utility room with full size washer and dryer. One or two small to medium size pets up to 35 pounds fully grown. $195 Lease Admin Fee.
