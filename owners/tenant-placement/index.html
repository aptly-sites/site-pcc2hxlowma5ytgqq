LEASING SUPPORT, DEFINED

The right start for your next lease.

Tenant placement focuses on the work between a vacant or soon-to-be-vacant home and a new lease. It can fit owners who want professional help finding and leasing to a resident while retaining responsibility for ongoing management.

Every property has its own timing, condition, and competition. Blue Crown starts with the address and your goals, then confirms the placement services and handoff in a written agreement.

HOW PLACEMENT WORKS

From rental-ready
to lease-ready.

A practical sequence for your home, with the final scope confirmed before work begins.

01 /

Position the rent

Review the home's address, condition, features, and competing rentals to choose an asking rent and launch plan.

02 /

Prepare the listing

Confirm property details, images, availability, and the information prospective residents need before they tour or apply.

03 /

Market and show

Promote the available home, respond to inquiries, and coordinate opportunities for prospective residents to see it.

04 /

Review applications

Use Blue Crown's online application process and published selection criteria. Completed applications are presented for the owner's final decision.

05 /

Complete the lease

Coordinate the agreed lease documents, signatures, and move-in requirements with the approved applicant.

06 /

Hand off clearly

Confirm the documents, dates, and responsibilities that pass back to you when the placement work is complete.

THE HANDOFF MATTERS

Focused help for the leasing phase.

Blue Crown's placement work is centered on preparing, marketing, and leasing the home. Once the agreed handoff is complete, you take over the ongoing owner responsibilities described in your agreement.

GOOD QUESTIONS. CLEAR ANSWERS.

Before you hand us the keys.

The details of a placement depend on the property and the service agreement. These answers are a starting point for your conversation with Blue Crown.

How is tenant placement different from full management?

Placement is focused on getting a home leased and completing the agreed handoff. Full management adds ongoing support after the resident moves in. Blue Crown will confirm which responsibilities belong to each service in your agreement.

How are applicants reviewed?

Applicants use Blue Crown's online application and published selection criteria. Completed applications for the same property are presented to the owner for a final decision. Read the selection criteria.

How long will it take to find a resident?

There is no single timeline. Asking rent, property condition, location, season, and applicant activity all affect the time to lease. The team can discuss a plan for your specific address.

What does tenant placement cost?

Ask Blue Crown for a proposal for your property. The team will explain the placement scope and fees before you enter an agreement.
