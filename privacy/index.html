Privacy Policy

Last updated September 22, 2026

Blue Crown Property Management ("Blue Crown," "we," or "us") respects the privacy of property owners, prospective and current residents, clients, and visitors to this website. This policy explains the information we collect, how we use and share it, and how to contact us about it.

Information we collect

When you contact us or request a rental analysis, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, rental property address, property details, goals, and the information you choose to put in a message. The calculators on this website may also collect the property address and figures you enter if you submit a lead request. We receive listing inquiries and information you provide through applications, leases, owner or resident portals, other real-estate forms, and communications with our team.

Depending on the service you request, information may include rental history, employment or income information, payment details, tax forms, government identification, and information in a consumer report. A screening or background report is obtained only in connection with an authorized application or another authorized purpose. Please use the designated application or portal for sensitive identity or financial information; do not put it in a general website message.

Our website and service providers may also receive routine technical information, such as your browser, device, IP address, pages requested, and approximate location derived from your connection.

How we use information

We use information to respond to questions; prepare property-specific rental reviews; discuss and provide management, leasing, brokerage, and resident services; process applications and transactions; communicate about a property or account; maintain business records; meet reporting obligations; and improve our website and support. Contact information may be used to follow up on the request you submitted. A website inquiry does not enroll you in SMS marketing.

For prospective residents, information may be used to evaluate an application, request an authorized credit or background report, verify rental history, and discuss an application with the property owner. For property owners, we use information to provide management services, arrange authorized payments or deposits, and complete required tax or other reports. For clients in a transaction, we may share information with a service provider at the client's direction when needed to obtain the requested service.

When information is shared

Blue Crown does not sell or trade personal information. We may share it with people who need it to perform the services described above, including our authorized staff and agents, application and screening processors, property owners where relevant to a tenancy, payment and portal providers, and service providers that operate this website or our business. We may also disclose information at your direction, to comply with legal process, or to protect rights, property, or safety.

Access to application and transaction records is limited to people whose work requires it. Property owners do not receive general access to Blue Crown's files; application or screening information may be discussed with them for a property they own, and copies are provided when the applicant consents or when a lawful management transition requires transfer to the owner, counsel, or a new manager.

Website forms and outside services

Owner inquiries and rental-analysis requests submitted here are sent to Blue Crown's Aptly owner-lead system for follow-up. Current rental listings may be supplied by Aptly. Application, tour, owner-portal, resident-portal, maintenance, virtual-tour, school, and map features may open or embed services operated by other providers. Those providers may collect information under their own privacy policies when you use their tools. Review their terms before submitting sensitive information.

Maps on this website may load tiles from OpenStreetMap or an embedded Google map. These services receive the technical requests needed to display a map. External listing photos and virtual tours may likewise be requested from their hosts.

Cookies and browser choices

Cookies and similar browser storage can help a website or its service providers remember a session, operate a feature, and understand site activity. Your browser may let you block or remove cookies; doing so can affect portals, embedded tools, or other third-party features. The portal and embedded-service providers may use their own cookies and offer their own controls. This website's general pages can be browsed without submitting a form.

Safeguards, retention, and correction

Blue Crown uses reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect records and limit access to people with a business need. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information as needed for the requested service, business records, legal obligations, and dispute resolution, then dispose of it using reasonable methods appropriate to the record.

If you believe information in a Blue Crown record is wrong, contact us so we can review it. If we determine that information we furnished to a consumer reporting agency was inaccurate, we will seek to correct our records and notify the agency as appropriate. You may also contact us to ask about access to, correction of, or deletion of information, subject to records we must keep and other applicable requirements.

Children, communications, and updates

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit their personal information through this website. If you receive a marketing email from Blue Crown, you can use its unsubscribe instructions or contact us to stop those messages. Service or transaction communications may still be sent when needed.

We may update this policy as our services or practices change. The current version and its update date will appear on this page.
