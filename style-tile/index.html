TYPE GUIDELINES

Heading Style & Capitalization

Use Manrope for headings. Choose the heading level by page structure. Use Title Case for titles and labels; use sentence case for complete-sentence headlines and intentional campaign taglines.

TOPIC TITLE · NO PERIOD

Property Management in Dallas

Use Title Case for short labels, names, and topic headings. Capitalize major words; lowercase short articles, conjunctions, and prepositions unless they begin or end the heading. Preserve official casing for brand names, locations, and acronyms.

COMPLETE SENTENCE · PUNCTUATE

Find a home that feels like yours.

Use sentence case for a complete-sentence headline or an intentional campaign tagline, with terminal punctuation. End a direct question with a question mark.

Short titles and labels use Title Case with no terminal punctuation. For listing names supplied by Aptly, enter them in Title Case in Aptly. CSS heading scale: H1 44–76px · H2 32–48px · H3 23px.

BACKGROUND PATTERN GUIDELINES

Pattern Background Sizing

The Blue Crown pattern SVG has a 1:2 width-to-height ratio. Use background-size: 160px 320px as the standard tile size. This renders each B mark at about 60 × 72px. On compact layouts, use 120px 240px for marks around 45 × 54px.

Company team portraits: alternate the Harbor Fog field with Mist marks and the Bright Vellum #FDF8EC field with subtle Crown Gold #F0AF2D marks. The two SVGs are blue-crown-pattern-harbor-fog-mist.svg and blue-crown-pattern-vellum-crown-gold.svg.

To change the mark size, scale both background dimensions by the same proportion. Smaller dimensions make the B marks smaller and the pattern repeat more often. Keep the 1:2 ratio so the artwork is not stretched.

LINK GUIDELINES

When Links Open in a New Tab

Default: keep internal links in the current tab. This includes primary and global navigation and ordinary links to other pages on the site.

Open in a new tab:

  • All links to external websites, including external links in navigation menus, third-party portals, and hosted forms.
  • Links to internal form pages or form sections. If the form opens in an overlay modal, keep it in the current tab.
  • Links to individual property detail pages.
  • Links to calculator pages.
  • Links to PDFs and downloadable files.

These listed destinations are exceptions to the internal-link default, wherever the link appears. For new-tab links, use target="_blank" with rel="noopener noreferrer". For downloadable files, the browser may download the file rather than display it in a new tab.

Make new-tab behavior clear before activation: show the ↗ cue on new-tab links, except when the link’s visual content is a logo or image; omit the visible arrow for those links and retain accessible text such as “opens in a new tab” for assistive technology.
